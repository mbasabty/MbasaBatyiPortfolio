A little about how I work

I'm a full-stack developer who likes taking messy ideas and turning them into products people actually enjoy using. Most of my work lives somewhere between a well-typed API and a UI that doesn't make you think.

Lately I've been deep in React, Node, and a healthy amount of Python for anything data-shaped. I care a lot about fast load times, readable code, and leaving things better than I found them.

When I'm not at the keyboard, I'm usually tinkering with a side project, fixing something around the house badly, or arguing about tabs vs. spaces (tabs, obviously).
